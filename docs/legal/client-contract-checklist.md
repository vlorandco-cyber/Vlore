# Client service agreement checklist

What a signed agreement (or signed proposal plus standard terms) should cover. The website's
Terms & Conditions only govern use of the website; they cannot stand in for these. Have an
Ontario lawyer prepare or review the template.

## Engagement

- [ ] **Parties:** your legal name and the client's legal name, signatories and authority.
- [ ] **Scope:** deliverables, channels, what is excluded, and how change requests are priced.
- [ ] **Revisions:** number of rounds per deliverable, and what counts as a new request.
- [ ] **Approvals:** who approves, deadlines, and the effect of silence or late approval.
- [ ] **Timing:** milestones, dependencies on client inputs (content, access, feedback), and what happens to dates when inputs are late.
- [ ] **Client responsibilities:** accurate information, rights to materials supplied, legal compliance of their offers and claims, and product or regulatory approvals (e.g. health or financial claims).

## Money

- [ ] **Fees and taxes:** fixed fee or retainer or hourly rates, and HST/GST (and whether you are registered).
- [ ] **Ad spend:** paid directly by the client to the platform, or through you? If through you, how it is held, reconciled and reported.
- [ ] **Third-party costs:** software, plugins, themes, fonts, stock, hosting, domains and AI usage, and who pays and holds each account.
- [ ] **Payment terms:** invoicing schedule, due dates, late payment, and suspension for non-payment.
- [ ] **Deposits, cancellation and refunds:** a business decision for you and your lawyer. Don't copy one from another agency.

## Ownership and access

- [ ] **IP ownership and licensing:** when ownership of deliverables passes (e.g. on full payment). Your pre-existing tools, templates and code stay yours, under a licence to the client. Third-party assets are covered by their own licences.
- [ ] **Portfolio rights:** permission to show the work, the client's name and results, or a confidentiality opt-out.
- [ ] **Account access:** the client owns ad accounts, Google Business Profile, analytics, domains, CRM and email accounts. You get user access, not ownership. Cover removal of access at the end.
- [ ] **Credentials:** how they are shared (a password manager, not email), multi-factor authentication, and least-privilege roles.

## Data and confidentiality

- [ ] **Confidentiality:** mutual obligations, duration and exceptions.
- [ ] **Security:** reasonable safeguards, incident notice to the client, and cooperation.
- [ ] **Client data processing:** when you handle the client's customer data (CRM, email lists, ad audiences, forms, AI tools), you act on the client's instructions only, for the client's purposes. The client is responsible for having consent (PIPEDA, CASL for its email lists).
- [ ] **Data processing agreement (DPA):** **recommended** whenever you access or host client customer data in CRM, email or AI work. It covers instructions, confidentiality, safeguards, sub-processors, breach notification, return or deletion at the end, and help with access requests. Required wording varies (Quebec Law 25, U.S. and EU clients), so have a lawyer confirm.
- [ ] **Subcontractors:** whether you may use them, confidentiality flow-down, and responsibility for their work.
- [ ] **AI use:** which AI tools may be used, whether client or customer data may be entered into them, review of AI-generated content, and IP and accuracy limits of AI output.
- [ ] **CASL for client campaigns:** the client confirms consent for its lists; your role in unsubscribe handling.

## Risk and ending

- [ ] **No guaranteed results:** rankings, leads, revenue and ROAS depend on factors outside your control (matches the website terms).
- [ ] **Platform dependencies:** platform policy changes, ad disapprovals, suspensions and outages.
- [ ] **Warranties:** what you do warrant (e.g. professional skill and care, original work), and disclaimers.
- [ ] **Liability:** any cap (amount or basis), excluded damage types, and carve-outs. A lawyer's decision; nothing is set on the website.
- [ ] **Indemnities:** mutual and limited (e.g. the client's materials and claims, your infringement). Avoid blanket indemnities.
- [ ] **Insurance:** professional liability or E&O, and cyber cover, especially if offering security services.
- [ ] **Term and termination:** notice periods, termination for breach, and what is payable on early termination.
- [ ] **Handover:** delivery of files, code and credentials, transfer of accounts, return or deletion of client data, and post-termination support.
- [ ] **Disputes and law:** Ontario law, a forum or mediation step (a lawyer's choice), and notices.
